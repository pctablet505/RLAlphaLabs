Working With Us:
the Honest Picture

Read this before you write. It is short, and it says what this is and what it is not.

What this is

  • One founder. You would work directly with the founder. There is no other team, no manager and no office yet.
  • A few collaborators at a time. We work with one to three people at once. We are not building a headcount.
  • Research stage. We trade on paper only. No real money is traded, and there is no revenue yet.
  • Remote and asynchronous. No fixed office or time zone. Some overlap for calls helps.

Who we are looking for

  • Quantitative researchersStatistics, market microstructure, Indian markets
  • Machine-learning engineersLanguage models on documents, evaluation without leakage
  • Data and infrastructure engineersMarket data, reliable pipelines

See the four open problems, with who each one suits →

How the work runs

  • Every test starts with a written design, frozen before any result is read. The design says in advance what a failure looks like.
  • Negative results are recorded, not shelved.
  • Costs are counted as we would really pay them: taxes, fees and the spread.
  • AI agents from several vendors do much of the engineering, under a written rulebook.

The setup

  • Minute data for about 750 NSE stocks since 2015.
  • A 96 GB GPU, and language models served on our own hardware.
  • Order-book snapshots recorded during market hours.
  • A codebase with 37,260 automated tests, and a written record of every decision.

How it starts

  1. You writeTell us what you would work on, and show us something you have built. We share: a reply within a week.
  2. We talkA conversation, to see if the problem and the way we work suit you. We share: how the problem came up, what we have tried, and how we test.
  3. A first problem, togetherSmall and well defined, so we both learn how the other works. We share: the methods and data that problem needs.

We read every message and reply within a week.
