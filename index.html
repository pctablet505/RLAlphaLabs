Forensic Validation · Why Most Retail Backtests Lie

The 8 Backtest Traps We Caught and Eliminated

Each trap below inflated simulated Indian equity returns by +0.3%/yr to +29.6%/yr before our invariant audits caught and sealed it. Click any row to inspect the failure mode and our engine rule.

01 Broker Store Survivorship Bias +2.3% to +6.0%/yr phantom alpha

Standard broker APIs drop delisted tokens. Selecting a universe from today's active list silently erases 1,142 dead NSE companies (like Satyam, DHFL, or Future Retail) and inflates annual returns by up to 6 percentage points.

Engine RuleEvery historical rebalance queries our 6,632-archive point-in-time panel strictly as-of session t, pricing terminal delistings explicitly.

02 Same-Bar Close Decision & Fill +8.0% to +29.6%/yr phantom alpha

Computing a signal from day t's closing price and filling the trade at that exact same close captures a phantom microstructure bounce that no live order can ever achieve.

Engine RuleMandatory 1-session execution lag: signals computed after close t execute at session t+1 with upper/lower circuit-band lock checks.

03 Quarter-End vs. Broadcast Timestamp Leakage +0.75%/yr phantom alpha

Assuming Q4 financials are known on March 31 (or on the statutory deadline) leaks future earnings weeks before the company actually broadcasts its XBRL filing to the exchange.

Engine RuleEvery fundamental record is gated by knowable_at = max(received_ts, broadcast_ts) mapped to the next NSE trading session after 15:30 IST.

04 Ignoring the Flat ₹15.34 CDSL DP Charge +30.7 bps/sell @ ₹5k ticket

Modelling delivery costs as a flat percentage (e.g., 10–20 bps) hides the ₹15.34 per-scrip depository sell fee—which adds +30.7 bps on a ₹5,000 position and destroys small-capital multi-stock portfolios.

Engine RuleFull per-fill statutory waterfall with exact ₹15.34 DP debit on delivery sells and NSE dated tick-size floors.

05 Illegal Tax-Lot Selection & Pre-Tax Comparisons +0.30% to +3.0%/yr tax omission

Comparing a high-turnover strategy pre-tax against an index, or using Highest-Cost-First (HCF) lot accounting, hides the 20.8% STCG tax paid every rebalance. Indian law (s.45(2A)) mandates FIFO per demat account.

Engine RuleBitemporal FIFO demat tax-lot accounting with historical Finance Act rates, 8-year loss carry-forward, and terminal liquidation tax.

06 High-Churn Random Baselines +1.0% to +1.4%/yr false flattery

Comparing a low-turnover factor portfolio against a naive random-stock baseline that replaces 100% of its holdings every quarter makes the candidate look good solely because the random baseline burned 1.4%/yr in extra fees.

Engine RuleAll null controls are persistence-, turnover-, and liquidity-tercile matched to the candidate strategy.

07 Overlapping-Window Sign-Share Illusions 50.15% false-positive rate

Counting how many rolling 3-year or 5-year windows are positive ("beat the market in 73% of rolling windows") treats 95%-shared data as independent—producing a 50.15% false-positive rate under zero true skill.

Engine RuleDisjoint fold evaluation, Newey-West HAC standard errors, and noncentral-t 80%-power MDE gates.

08 LLM Entity & Price Memorization Lookahead via pre-training

Feeding raw historical news or filings to a modern LLM allows it to recognize the company name, year, and stock price from its pre-training corpus—leaking future returns disguised as "sentiment analysis."

Engine RuleAutomated entity, date, and price scrubber replaces all tickers/ISINs with [FILER], dates with [D-30], and strips market-quote clauses before inference.
