Privacy

Privacy

What happens to what you send us. Short and plain.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send. The contact box puts your message, and the role and topic if you chose them, into an email in your own mail app. The site itself keeps nothing.

How it reaches us

When you press Send, your own mail app opens with the message ready. You send it from there, straight to our mailbox. No form service is involved, and this site has no server or database of its own. The copy buttons put the message or our address on your clipboard, and nowhere else.

If you message us on LinkedIn instead, the message stays on LinkedIn, under LinkedIn’s own terms.

Who sees it

The founder only. There is no team or contractor with access to messages.

What it is used for

Only to reply to you. It is not added to a mailing list, sold, or shared with anyone else.

How long we keep it

As long as the conversation needs it. To see what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, ask the same way you wrote to us, and we will do it.

Other services involved

Your mail provider and ours carry the email on its way to us. Like any website, the host and the font service see your IP address when your browser loads a page.

Your browser

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ad trackers. If you switch the colour theme, your choice is saved in your own browser only.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page changes first. This notice describes how a small, static site works today; it is not legal advice.
