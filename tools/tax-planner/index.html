Where Lot-Level Planning Actually Matters (and Where It Doesn't)

Instead of cherry-picking one showcase portfolio, we simulated 999 Indian SIP + lumpsum mutual fund portfolios (2–8 funds each, 0.5–8 year histories, median ₹38.7 lakh) and compared the optimizer against three by-hand methods using the same statutory calculator. Honest reading of the numbers: On withdrawals of ₹5 lakh or more (556 exact-target cases), the median reduction in this year's combined tax, exit load, and STT is ₹26,294 vs. redeeming from one fund (1.87% of cash raised; ≥ ₹5,000 in 85.4% of cases) and ₹25,042 vs. equal-share (1.86%; ≥ ₹5,000 in 89.7%). Because the solver minimizes total friction, it occasionally pays a small exit load (e.g. 1%) to avoid a much larger tax bill (20.8% STCG / 31.2% debt slab). Under ₹2 lakh, the ₹1.25 lakh annual LTCG exemption already covers most gain — do not pay for optimization on small withdrawals. A careful person sorting every FIFO lot by hand lands within ₹500 of the solver in 57.5% of all 988 withdrawals (568/988), and in 36.7% of withdrawals ≥ ₹5 lakh (204/556; median gap ₹1,563). Almost all of the reduction is this year's tax — part of which is deferred until remaining higher-gain units are sold later, unless future annual exemptions absorb it. Median solve time: 60.8 ms across the 988 clean cases (61.6 ms across all 999). In 11 of 999 cases the solver redeemed above target cash to harvest a loss lot (9 by 1.7%–32.5%, plus 2 within 0.5%: +₹2,020 and +₹12,695), so we excluded all 11 from the like-for-like table above and expose an optional overshoot cap in the solver.

Current Scope & Known Limits (Read Before Ordering)

We publish limits openly. Today, the engine covers resident individuals and HUFs in flat marginal tax slabs for FY2026-27 (Income-tax Act, 2025), including proportional Section 86 (old s.54F) ₹10 crore reinvestment-cap arithmetic and lot-date-effective exit load schedules. It does not yet auto-model NRI TDS/basic-exemption restrictions, progressive slab jumps / s.156 (old s.87A) rebate cliffs under ₹12 lakh total income, or multi-year residential-house lock-in conditions under s.86 (s.54F). Direct self-serve retail access is intentionally held back pending legal review under SEBI Investment Advisers Regulations; pilot memos are prepared for practicing CAs and financial professionals who verify final ITR schedules. RL Alpha Labs currently operates as an unregistered individual research lab (check invoicing and GST terms before placing paid orders).

Important Disclosure: This service computes deterministic capital-gains, exit-load, and STT arithmetic strictly from holdings supplied by the client or their CA. It never recommends securities or mutual fund schemes to buy, sell, or hold, and is not SEBI-registered investment advice or legal tax counsel. A practicing Chartered Accountant must review and confirm all calculations before submitting redemptions or filing returns. See our privacy notice for message handling.
