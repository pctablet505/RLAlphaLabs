Where Lot-Level Planning Actually Matters (and Where It Doesn't)

Instead of cherry-picking one showcase portfolio, we simulated 999 Indian SIP + lumpsum mutual fund portfolios (2–8 funds each, 0.5–8 year histories, median ₹38.7 lakh) and compared the optimizer against three by-hand methods using the same statutory calculator. Honest reading of the numbers: On withdrawals of ₹5 lakh or more (557 cases), the median reduction in this year's tax, exit load, and STT is ₹26,294 vs. redeeming from one fund and ₹25,065 vs. equal-share (~1.9% of cash raised; ≥ ₹5,000 in 85%–90% of cases). Under ₹2 lakh, the ₹1.25 lakh annual LTCG exemption already covers most gain — do not pay for optimization on small withdrawals. A careful person sorting every FIFO lot in a spreadsheet lands within ₹500 of the solver in 57% of cases (median gap ₹1,565 on ₹5L+). Almost all of the reduction is this year's tax — part of which is deferred until remaining higher-gain units are sold later, unless future annual exemptions absorb it. Median solve time: 62 ms; in 9 of 999 cases the solver redeemed above the target cash to harvest a loss lot, so we excluded those from like-for-like totals and flag over-redemption explicitly.

Current Scope & Known Limits (Read Before Ordering)

We publish limits openly. Today, the engine covers resident individuals and HUFs in flat marginal tax slabs for FY2026-27 (Income-tax Act, 2025). It does not yet auto-model NRI TDS/basic-exemption restrictions, progressive slab jumps / s.156 (old s.87A) rebate cliffs under ₹12 lakh total income, or Section 86 (old s.54F) ₹10 crore proportional reinvestment caps. Direct self-serve retail access is intentionally held back pending legal review under SEBI Investment Advisers Regulations; pilot memos are prepared for practicing CAs and financial professionals who verify final ITR schedules.

Important Disclosure: This service computes deterministic capital-gains, exit-load, and STT arithmetic strictly from holdings supplied by the client or their CA. It never recommends securities or mutual fund schemes to buy, sell, or hold, and is not SEBI-registered investment advice or legal tax counsel. A practicing Chartered Accountant must review and confirm all calculations before submitting redemptions or filing returns.
